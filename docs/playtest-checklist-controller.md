# Playtest checklist: controller and the new controls (2026-11-22)

Tick each line. Write down anything that feels wrong, even if it "works".
Names below are for an Xbox pad; PlayStation: X = Square, Y = Triangle, A = Cross,
B = Circle, LB/RB = L1/R1, LT/RT = L2/R2, View = Share, Menu = Options.

## 1. Plugging in
- [ ] Start the game with the controller already plugged in: it works at once.
- [ ] Unplug it during play: the log (Escape menu → Log) says "Controller disconnected",
      and the keyboard and mouse still work.
- [ ] Plug it back in: the log says "Controller connected: …" with its name.
- [ ] Touch the controller: the buttons shown on the ability wheel (bottom right) change
      to X / Y / A / B. Touch the mouse or keyboard: they change back to Left click /
      Right click / Q / Shift.

## 2. Moving and aiming
- [ ] The left stick walks in any direction, not only 8.
- [ ] Pushing the stick halfway walks slower than pushing it fully.
- [ ] WASD still walks as before.
- [ ] The right stick turns the hero to face where it points.
- [ ] Escape menu → Gameplay test → **Controller aim: Auto-lock**. Walk toward a wolf without
      touching the right stick: the hero turns to face it; attacks go at it.
- [ ] **Twin-stick**: without the right stick, attacks go where you walk.
- [ ] Fireball (or another Manual Aim ability): hold its button, aim with the right stick,
      let go. It lands where you aimed (further when the stick is pushed further), even in
      Auto-lock.

## 3. The ability wheel
- [ ] Four slots at the bottom right, each with its button shown: left (X / Left click) =
      your weapon, top (Y / Right click) = ranged attack, bottom (A / Q) = dash (Fire,
      Air) or Stone Skin (Earth), right (B / Shift) = the rest.
- [ ] New Earth hero: Rock Throw on Y, Stone Skin on A. New Air hero: Gust on Y.
- [ ] Equip a sword (or bow, hammer): its ability goes on X; whatever was there moves.
- [ ] Learn Flame Dash or Air Step: it goes on A.
- [ ] Drag an ability onto X yourself, then equip a weapon: your choice stays.
- [ ] Cooldowns sweep over the slot and count down.
- [ ] Drag an ability from the Spellbook (P) or the trees (T) onto a slot: it goes there.
- [ ] Drag an ability off the wheel and drop it elsewhere: the slot empties.
- [ ] Keyboard: Left click, Right click, Q and Shift fire the four slots.
- [ ] Controller: X, Y, A and B fire them.
- [ ] Using a targeted attack (Gust, Water Whip…) without targeting anyone: it goes to the
      enemy you point at (mouse) or aim at (controller).
- [ ] Healing Touch pointed at a friend heals them; pointed at nothing, heals you.

## 4. Timing bars
- [ ] Keyboard: Space plays the bar (tap green, hold blue).
- [ ] Controller: RT plays the bar; holding RT holds a blue zone.
- [ ] Right-click no longer plays the bar (it is the special now).

## 5. Other buttons
- [ ] E / LB: talk to people, pick up loot.
- [ ] Dead: E / LB releases to the graveyard.
- [ ] 1 / D-pad up drinks a health potion; 2 / D-pad down a mana potion.
- [ ] Tab / LT / D-pad right picks the next enemy.
- [ ] D-pad left clears the target and cancels an aim.
- [ ] R / RB says "Element powers aren't in the game yet".
- [ ] B / View opens the inventory.
- [ ] Esc / Menu: cancels your cast, closes menus, then opens the Escape menu.
- [ ] Escape menu → Controls lists every control for both devices.
- [ ] Right-click another player out of combat: Trade / Duel.

## 6. Online
- [ ] A friend on a controller walks and fights; you see them move smoothly at any angle.
