# Playthrough checklist: Act I, "The Floor Guild" (quests 1-01 to 1-06)

For a non-programmer. Play from a new character, in this order (1-03, 1-04 and 1-05
can be done in any order). Tick each box; note anything that reads wrong (every line
is in `content/dialogue/act1.json`). Debug shortcuts (`): `goto <zone>`, `level <n>`.
Expect about level 10 after everything here, before 1-07 The Silent Shaft (milestone 4).

## 1-01 The Guild Comes to Millbrook (level 1)
- [ ] In Millbrook, **Maren Voss** (blue, Floor Guild) stands by the south bridge
      with a "!". Her greeting has two questions; "How do I fight?" explains facing,
      the action bar, timing bars and stepping out of red marks.
- [ ] Accept: drive off **3 wolves and 2 boars** in the fields across the south bridge.
      Boars charge: a red tile marks where they will land; step off it.
- [ ] Hand in to Maren: XP, gold, 2 health potions, and a choice of Pebble Scroll
      (Earth), Open Palm Scroll (Air) or Hunter's Shortbow (Bow). She invites you to
      the Crossing.

## 1-02 Applicants Wanted (level 2)
- [ ] At the Crossing, Maren Voss waits at the Guild Hall (north-west). Accept.
- [ ] Talk to **Warden Dorne** at the hall door (he registers you) and to
      **Quartermaster Harrow** along the north wall (your Guild kit). Each says
      something of their own.
- [ ] Hand in to Maren: 2 mana potions and a choice of Spark Scroll (Fire), River
      Lash Scroll (Water) or Iron Mallet (Hammer). She lists the three trials.
- [ ] Harrow sells starter gear, potions and every tier-1 scroll and weapon.

## 1-03 Wells for the Windsteppe (level 3; from Maren)
- [ ] On the Windsteppe (west of the Crossing), the **Well-Digging Crew** waits by
      the track. Talk to them: they set off for Tsaga's Camp and only walk while you
      stay close. Iron wolves come for them.
- [ ] Let them die once: the quest **fails**, Maren offers it again ("try again").
- [ ] At the camp, kill **4 iron wolves**, then hand in to **Saran**: the wells work,
      "but the herds are still dying… it's the cold that takes them now".

## The White Death (side, level 4; from Saran after 1-03)
- [ ] Collect **4 iron wolf pelts** (they only drop while you're on the quest) and
      bring the **Winter Stores** (handed to you) to **Elder Tsaga**: the steppe
      spirits have gone quiet. Hand in to Saran: "the year the new mine road came
      through".

## 1-04 Grain for Deepcut (level 3; from the Guild Board)
- [ ] The **Guild Grain Wagon** waits at the Crossing end of the Road to Deepcut.
      Bandit Brutes lie in wait beside the road.
- [ ] Let the wagon be destroyed once: the quest fails and the board posts it again.
- [ ] Bring it to the Deepcut end, then hand in to **Steward Brann** in Deepcut:
      the mine owners will sell the grain on "at a fair price. Triple, I'd guess".

## Shift Bell (side, level 6; from the Mine Board after 1-04)
- [ ] Clear the rubble at the **Collapsed Shaft** (E), put down **3 Human Husks**
      (slow, heavy hitters), talk to **Miner Ostrik**: "the rock was silent before it
      fell". Hand it in to Ostrik (since milestone 4: Brann leaves Deepcut after 1-06).

## 1-05 Fever in Millbrook (level 3; from the Guild Board)
- [ ] You get 3 **Fever Draughts**. Talk to **Odo the Apothecary** (Crossing market),
      then give the draughts to **Healer Wenna** in Millbrook.
- [ ] Follow the grey river upstream and examine 3 glowing spots: in Millbrook (beside
      the grey river), on the Road to Deepcut (beside the stream) and in Deepcut (by
      the tailings pond): the silt "glitters amber… like crushed Sunstone".
- [ ] Hand in to Wenna: "Deepcut's mine. Of course it is."

## 1-06 Recognition (level 6; from Maren after 1-03, 1-04 and 1-05)
- [ ] Maren sends you to **Prince Caedan Ashvale** at the hall steps. He thanks you,
      then asks for volunteers: Deepcut's deep-seam miners "have gone silent".
      Rewards include a choice of Sun Warrior's Breath, Scroll of the Current or the
      Traveler's Longsword. Then 1-07 The Silent Shaft: see `playtest-checklist-act1-full.md`.

## Pins on the Map (side, level 7; from the Guild Board after 1-06)
- [ ] Visit Millbrook's grey river, Tsaga's Camp, the ambush on the Road to Deepcut
      and Deepcut's tailings pond, then use the **Guild Map** in the hall yard: "every
      pin sits on a Sunstone road". Hand in to Maren.

## Rewards and levels
- [ ] Over the act you can pick items from all seven trees (Fire, Water, Earth, Air,
      Sword, Bow, Hammer), all of them abilities that are in the game.
- [ ] After everything: about level 10.

## Party size (two to four players)
- [ ] With more players in a zone, its enemies have more health and hit a little
      harder (look at a wolf's health bar alone, then with friends). Elite bosses
      don't change.
- [ ] Each player who fought gets the same XP from a kill as a solo player would.
- [ ] Every quest above can be taken by one player and joined by the others through
      the "Join them?" prompt; escorts, deliveries, examined spots and kills count for
      everyone nearby.
