# Playtest checklist: Enemy Testing Grounds and the Salamander Hound

For a non-programmer. Tick each box; if something looks wrong, note what you did
and what you saw.

## Getting there
- [ ] In Millbrook, walk to the north-east corner of town (up-right on screen,
      past the stone house). A dirt road leads to a gap in the hedge.
- [ ] A red banner reads **Enemy Testing Grounds** next to the gap.
- [ ] Walk through the gap: you arrive in the Enemy Testing Grounds (the message
      log says "You enter Enemy Testing Grounds").
- [ ] Walk back out through the same gap (the bottom-left edge on screen, at the
      west end): you are back in Millbrook at the gate.
- [ ] Co-op: host a game, have a friend join, and both walk through the gate and
      back. Each of you can use it on your own; both of you see each other there.

## The map
- [ ] You start on a cobblestone plaza at the west end, with a **Reset Shrine**
      (a blue figure) and a graveyard.
- [ ] A dirt road runs east. North of it (up on screen) is a row of pens, each
      with one enemy and a red banner with its name and level: Wolf (Lv 1),
      Goblin Shaman (Lv 2), Salamander Hound (Lv 2), Bandit Brute (Lv 3), Fen
      Skirmisher (Lv 4).
- [ ] Walk the whole road to the east end: no enemy notices you while you stay
      on the road.
- [ ] At the far end a hedge with a gap on the road marks the **Boss Arena**;
      Old Grizzlehide stands inside on cracked earth (he doesn't patrol here).

## Fighting
- [ ] Walk into a pen: its enemy attacks. Fight it anywhere, even dragging it
      back and forth along the road until it gives up and runs home: no other
      enemy joins.
- [ ] Kill an enemy: about **10 seconds** later it is back in its pen.
- [ ] Talk to the Reset Shrine (stand next to it, press E): every enemy is back
      in its pen at full health, burning ground and markers disappear, your own
      buffs and debuffs are gone, and you are out of combat. The message log says
      "The shrine glows…".

## Salamander Hound
- [ ] It is a red fire lizard-hound, standing on its tile (feet on the tile
      centre, not floating). Idle, it puffs smoke and crouches now and then.
- [ ] It notices you from 5 tiles away and runs fast (faster than a wolf).
- [ ] **Cinder Pounce:** when you are 2–3 tiles away it crouches and a red
      **plus-shaped marker** (5 tiles) appears beside you on its side, with you
      standing on one arm of it. About half a second later it leaps onto the
      centre and bursts into fire: anyone still on the marker gets hurt.
- [ ] Step off the marker in time (sideways or away from it): you take no damage.
      It pounces again about every 8 seconds.
- [ ] **Bite:** next to you, it lunges and bites (a short red cast bar first).
- [ ] **Ember Trail:** while fighting, each tile it runs over glows orange for
      2 seconds; standing on one burns you a little.
- [ ] Hit it: its body flashes white (its shadow doesn't). Kill it: it sinks
      down, lies still, then fades away.
- [ ] Turn around it: facing you, away, left and right. **Known placeholder:** the
      hound has no back views yet, so facing away from the camera (up-left /
      up-right) shows its front views.
- [ ] Co-op: both players see the same pounce marker, the same trail and the same
      animations at the same time.
