# Playtest checklist: the new tileset

This covers only the new ground art. Characters, enemies and ability effects still
use placeholder shapes. Play solo first, then with a friend.

## Looks
- [ ] **Do:** start the game and look around Millbrook.
      **Expect:**
      - a leafy hedge around town
      - stone houses
      - a cobblestone plaza with the graveyard cross
      - dirt paths between Old Maren, Hilda and Captain Rowan
      - a small fountain
- [ ] **Do:** look closely at any tile.
      **Expect:** crisp, square pixels with no blur or shimmer, including while walking.
- [ ] **Do:** scroll the mouse wheel in and out.
      **Expect:** a few fixed zoom steps; every step stays crisp, and nothing is
      blurry halfway.
- [ ] **Do:** resize the window, or play full screen at 1080p.
      **Expect:** still crisp, and the HUD scales like it did before.

## Greenfields
- [ ] **Do:** walk off the east side of town into Greenfields.
      **Expect:**
      - grass fields with darker and lusher patches
      - a dirt path leading east
      - flowers here and there
- [ ] **Do:** compare a few stretches of grass and of path.
      **Expect:** the ground doesn't look like one tile repeated; each spot keeps
      its look when you come back.
- [ ] **Do:** look at the river.
      **Expect:**
      - the river sits a little lower than the banks
      - rocks stand in it
      - the two crossings are stone slabs you can walk over
      - a few water tiles twinkle
- [ ] **Do:** try to walk into the river, the pond or a log.
      **Expect:** you can't; flowers and weeds can be walked over.
- [ ] **Do:** walk to the north-east plateau and up the ramp.
      **Expect:** a raised rocky area with stone sides and brown boulders, 4 levels up.
- [ ] **Do:** find the pond in the far south corner.
      **Expect:** pale water with white foam where it touches land, and a rock in it.

## Hiding behind things
- [ ] **Do:** walk behind a boulder pillar or a boulder on the plateau.
      **Expect:** it hides the lower part of your hero.
- [ ] **Do:** stand so a tall boulder is between you and a Goblin Shaman.
      **Expect:** its bolts stop at the boulder, and it can't see you to aggro
      through it.
- [ ] **Do:** stand right next to a rock pillar and walk past it.
      **Expect:** no flicker; the pillar always covers the part of your hero behind it.

## Aiming on the new ground
- [ ] **Do:** aim Fire Dart or Fire Whip on grass, on the path and over the river.
      **Expect:** the blue tiles are easy to see, with a dark outline. Aiming out of
      range shows red.
- [ ] **Do:** let a Bandit Brute wind up Crushing Blow, and cast Burning Ground.
      **Expect:** the red warning and the orange burning area are easy to see on grass.
- [ ] **Do:** aim at a tile in the river or the pond.
      **Expect:** the highlight lies on the water surface, not floating above it.
